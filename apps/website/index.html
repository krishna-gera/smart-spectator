Features

Everything a home camera needs

Built for the phones you already have. One main phone sees everything; any number of spare phones become cameras.

Live video on your Wi-Fi

Hardware H.264 streaming straight to your main phone. Video never goes to a server.

AI watch tasks

“Tell me when the kettle stops steaming.” The AI checks the picture and alerts you when it happens.

Motion alerts

Motion detection runs on the camera phone, with optional AI descriptions like “a person at the door”.

Web dashboard

Open your cameras, events and AI tasks on any computer on the same Wi-Fi. Add it to your home screen.

Camera health

Battery, charging and connection for every camera — with alerts when a battery runs low or a camera drops off.

Power saver

Camera phones stay awake but black out the screen, so an old phone can watch all day.

Privacy

Your video stays home

VYRO is local-first. There is no VYRO account and no cloud video storage.

Live video stays on Wi-Fi

Cameras stream directly to your main phone. Events and snapshots are stored on the main phone.

AI only when you ask

Single pictures go to the AI service only for tasks or descriptions you turn on — and the camera shows an “AI watching” badge.

Codes, not passwords

Cameras join with their own code; the web dashboard needs a separate code; wrong guesses are locked out.

Web dashboard

Open your cameras on a computer

Your main phone runs the dashboard itself. On the main phone open VYRO → Settings → Web dashboard to find its address and sign-in code, then open it from any browser on the same Wi-Fi.

Tip: once it is open, use “Add to Home Screen” — the dashboard installs with the VYRO icon.

FAQ

Questions

Which phones can be cameras?

Any iPhone or Android phone that runs VYRO. Old phones work well — plug them in and turn on Power saver.

Do both phones need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

Yes. Video goes straight from the camera to the main phone over your network, which keeps it private and fast.

What if my Wi-Fi or the main phone's address changes?

Cameras find their main phone again by themselves. If a camera is removed on the main phone, it goes back to showing its code.

How does the AI work, and what does it cost?

For AI tasks and descriptions, VYRO sends a single, reduced picture to an online AI model through the VYRO AI service. Live video is never sent. The AI shares a per-minute budget, so several tasks take turns.

Can several main phones watch the same camera?

Yes. Type the same camera code on each main phone; the camera streams to all of them.

Does VYRO work when the app is closed?

Keep VYRO open on the main phone and the cameras. Camera phones stay awake and black out their screen to save power.
