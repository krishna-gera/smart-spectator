## 3. Motion Gating Efficiency

Motion gating measures pixel-level frame differences to bypass deep neural network inference on static scenes.

- **Sequence Length**: 60 frames (10 motion frames, 50 static frames)
- **Gated Frames**: 50 / 60 frames
- **Compute Reduction**: **83.3%**
- **Edge Thermal Benefit**: Significantly reduces sustained thermal dissipation and power draw during idle periods.
